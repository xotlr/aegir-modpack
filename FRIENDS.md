# Playing AEGIR

AEGIR is a Norse sea-and-sky survival world: Create ships, food that makes you stronger, and one
finite world that wraps around. Setup takes about ten minutes, once.

## Install (once)

1. Install **Prism Launcher**: <https://prismlauncher.org/download/>
2. Download **[AEGIR-instance.zip](https://github.com/xotlr/aegir-modpack/raw/main/dist/AEGIR-instance.zip)**.
3. In Prism: **Add Instance → Import → choose the zip → OK**.
4. **Accounts** (top right) → **Add Microsoft** and log in with the account that owns Minecraft.
5. Double-click **AEGIR**. The first start downloads the pack (about 600 MB) and takes a few
   minutes; later starts only download what changed. Keep Prism open while it works.

Your normal Minecraft keybinds, mouse settings, FOV, sound and server list are copied over
automatically on the first start.

**Memory:** the instance uses 8 GB. With 16 GB of RAM or more that is right. With less, change it in
Prism: right-click AEGIR → Edit → Settings → Memory → Maximum 6144 MB.

## Good to know

- **Health is food.** You start with 3 hearts. Eating adds hearts for a while (better meals add more
  and last longer); up to three foods count at once.
- **Death is sad but cheap.** Your pets wait at your grave and wander around it until you come back.
  Opening your own grave gives you a short burst of strength. Dying twice within ten minutes does
  not cost skill levels twice.
- **`/tt`** opens a menu to travel to anyone in your team. Others: `/tpa <name>`.
- **Music discs:** put a SoundCloud or direct MP3 link on a blank disc at the Etching Table. Everyone
  near the jukebox hears it.
- **Voice chat:** press **V** to set it up the first time.
- **Shaders** are off by default. To turn them on: Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs →
  Complementary Reimagined (pick the Euphoria version). Costs a lot of FPS.

## What talks to the internet

- Prism and the pack's updater download mods from Modrinth, CurseForge and GitHub.
- Music discs fetch the link that is on them (SoundCloud, Bandcamp or the file's own site).
- Villager chat AI is **off**. Nothing you say in chat leaves the server.

## Something broke?

Prism → right-click AEGIR → **Folder** → `logs` → send Ronald `latest.log`.
